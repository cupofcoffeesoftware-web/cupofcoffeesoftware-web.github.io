Overview

Apples of Gold is a Bible reading tracker. You can use it without an account, and then everything stays on your device. If you sign in to use Reading Groups, the app stores a small amount of data online so your group can work. We don't sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking.

Without an Account

If you don't sign in, Apples of Gold collects nothing. Your reading plans, progress, reading history, reflections, homestead, and reminder settings are stored only on your device. They are deleted when you delete the app.

Backups you make with Export Backup are files you save yourself. We never receive them.

Reading Groups (Optional Sign-In)

Reading Groups need Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google. When you sign in and use groups, we store:

  • Account details: an account ID, plus the email address and name that Apple or Google provide. If you use Hide My Email, we only see Apple's relay address.
  • Your nickname: by default it's your first name. You can change it, and other members of your groups see it.
  • Group information: names of groups you create or join, invite codes, and any reading plan your group shares, including a custom plan.
  • Reading progress you choose to share: if you turn on Share progress, your group sees whether you read today and how far you are in the shared plan. You can turn this off at any time.
  • Gifts and the online homestead: the gifts you send and receive, and the reading days and homestead actions used to verify them.

Only members of a group can see that group's name, nicknames, and shared progress. Your reflections are never uploaded. When you share a reflection card, it goes only where you send it with the share sheet.

This data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Cloud Functions), which processes it for us to run the service.

Weather

If you're signed in and allow location access, the homestead shows your local weather. Your approximate location (to about 3 km) is sent to Apple Weather to get the forecast. We don't store it on our servers.

Notifications

Daily reminders are scheduled on your device. No data is sent anywhere to deliver them.

Deleting Your Data

In Reading Groups → Account → Delete account, you can delete your account at any time. This deletes your account and the groups you own. It also removes your membership, shared progress, and gift data from other groups. Data stored only on your device stays there until you delete the app.

Children's Privacy

Apples of Gold is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to This Policy
